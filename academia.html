Publications & Preprints
Preprint · April 2026

Invariant measures for the open KPZ equation: the Gaussian case

Building on a recent paper of Gu and Quastel for the full-line KPZ equation, this paper adapts their Stein's-equation approach to the open KPZ equation. I show that for boundary conditions of the form $$\partial_x h(t,0) = \partial_x h(t,1) = \alpha, \qquad \alpha \in \mathbb{R},$$ Brownian motion with constant drift $\alpha$ is an invariant measure of the dynamics, up to a global height shift.

arXiv:2604.23462 · Submitted to Mathematical Physics, Analysis and Geometry
Selected Talks
Summer 2024 · Reading group on KPZ

Introduction to Random Tilings

A motivated introduction to random lozenge tilings of polygonal domains on the triangular lattice, culminating in a proof of Thurston's theorem on tileability conditions.

Fall 2023 · Seminar course on discrete geometry

Kasteleyn's Theorem

An intuitive proof of Kasteleyn's 1961 theorem showing that dimer model partition functions on finite planar graphs are Pfaffians of weighted adjacency matrices.

Winter 2023 · Reading course on hyperbolic geometry

Constructing Compact Hyperbolic Surfaces

A proof that compact boundaryless hyperbolic surfaces of genus $g \geq 2$ are uniquely determined by their Fenchel–Nielsen parameters.
